## 🧭 About Me

I work at the intersection of **data and physical commodity markets**, focusing on how real-world supply flows behave under disruption.

My work is centered around breaking complex market systems into measurable components — flows, routing signals, and regional exposure — rather than relying on price alone.

Recently, I built a live **supply intelligence system** tracking the Strait of Hormuz crisis using AIS vessel data, LNG price spreads, and macro supply indicators.

### 🚢 Gulf Crisis Supply Intelligence System
Live system analyzing global energy disruption dynamics
